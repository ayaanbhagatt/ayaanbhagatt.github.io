# Thinking Outside the Box

the goal was to make 4 different objects with 4 different uses out of 1 cardboard box

the goal was also to think like an engineer and expand my creativity

here's what i came up with

## 1 - Amp Pad

![]({{ '/IMG_1618(1).jpg' | relative_url }}){: .small-image }

My cousin plays guitar often in his garage or other dusty spaces. The amp pad solves this, protecting expensive equipment from the floor.

## 2 - Catch-All Tray

![]({{ '/IMG_1619(1).jpg' | relative_url }}){: .small-image }

My sister's room is very messy, and as a result there's random stuff everywhere. The catch all fixes this as it serves as a place to keep all miscellaneous objects.

## 3 - Camera Cover

![]({{ '/IMG_1622(1).jpg' | relative_url }}){: .small-image }

You never know who's spying on you through your camera. The camera cover solves this as it shields both your camera and your privacy.

## 4 - Cap Display Stand

![]({{ '/IMG_1621(1).jpg' | relative_url }}){: .small-image }

Ever wanna display your awesome caps but you can't find a good way to? The cap model solves this problem exactly, offering you a cool way to display your caps at home.
